Privacy Policy

ClearRound Pro · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy covers ClearRound Pro, its worker-sheet website and related services. For the Standard app, read the ClearRound Privacy Policy. Privacy enquiries can be sent to ClearRound, United Kingdom, at mail@clearroundapp.com.

Worker Sharing uses online services provided by Google Firebase and Google Cloud, separately from your private Apple iCloud data. Creating a worker sheet sends selected job information to these services so your worker can access it and return updates.

1. Your business records

You may enter customer names, addresses, postcodes, telephone numbers, email addresses, property photographs and notes; business details; round names and assignments; jobs, instructions, schedules and completion dates; quotes, prices, payment status and methods; income, expenses and receipt photographs. These records are stored on your device using SwiftData and may synchronise through your private Apple iCloud/CloudKit account.

Entering a record in the app and sharing a job online are separate actions. Worker Sharing processes the information included in the selected sheet; it is not simply private iCloud synchronisation.

2. Who is responsible for information?

The business using Pro decides why and how to use its customer and worker information and is generally the controller of those records. It is responsible for a lawful basis, appropriate privacy information, accuracy and sharing only what is needed. When we host selected records and process worker responses on that business’s instructions, we provide processing services for that business. ClearRound is responsible for its own account administration, support and service-security processing.

3. Worker Sharing accounts and Firebase Authentication

Worker Sharing uses Firebase Authentication for owner email/password accounts, email verification and sign-in. Firebase processes the account email address, user identifier, authentication credentials and verification status. Authentication also involves session or token information and technical information such as IP addresses needed to operate and protect the service. A worker opening a shared sheet does not need a separate app account.

We use account information to identify the owner, restrict access to their sharing tools, verify their email and help protect the service. Never send your password to support.

4. Shared sheets and worker responses

When an owner creates a sheet, Firebase/Google Cloud services store and deliver the selected information. Depending on the jobs and sharing options, this can include customer names, addresses and contact details, job descriptions and instructions, scheduled dates, round details and identifiers. Prices and payment information are shared with workers only when the owner enables the relevant financial-sharing option.

Workers can submit job outcomes, completion or postponement dates and other supported updates. Where financial sharing is enabled, responses can include payment status, amounts and payment method. Responses are returned for the owner to review and apply to business records.

The service also stores information needed to operate and secure sharing, including owner/user identifiers, share and job identifiers, link-token hashes, sessions, expiry and revocation information, submitted updates and review/application decisions.

Treat a worker link as confidential. A person who obtains an active link may be able to view the sheet and submit updates. Share it only with the intended worker. Expiry or revocation limits further access; it cannot retrieve information someone has already copied, downloaded or photographed.

5. Service providers and technical information

  • Apple: device storage and security, iCloud/CloudKit synchronisation, App Store purchases and subscription entitlement information.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: Firebase Authentication, Firebase Hosting for the worker website, Cloud Functions for sharing requests, and the associated backend storage and infrastructure used for sheets and responses.

Hosting, authentication and cloud infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser/device information, request timestamps, service identifiers, errors and security or operational logs. This supports delivery, troubleshooting, abuse prevention and reliability. Provider service data and backup retention can differ from the retention of app records.

Provider information is available in Firebase Privacy and Security, the Google Cloud Privacy Notice and Apple’s privacy information. We do not sell customer lists or use them for third-party behavioural advertising.

6. Why we process information

We process owner account information to provide the requested account and sharing service, support communications to respond to enquiries, and technical information to deliver and secure the service. Where we are the controller, our bases are performance of our contract for requested services, legitimate interests in support, service reliability and preventing misuse, and legal obligations where applicable. Where consent is required for a particular optional use, it can be withdrawn without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. The business using Pro determines its own lawful basis for customer and worker records.

7. Payments, permissions and other services

Apple processes App Store subscription purchases. Pro uses purchase and entitlement information to determine access to paid features; it does not receive complete payment-card details from Apple. Recording a customer’s cash, card or other payment method is a business record, not payment-card processing. Do not put card numbers or security codes in notes.

Optional photos, camera access and notifications use relevant device permissions, which you can manage in Settings. Apple performs Face ID or device authentication; Pro does not receive your biometric template. Opening a map, sending a message or exporting a file passes the information needed for that action to the service or destination you choose. Protect exported copies.

8. Retention and deletion

Local and iCloud records: you manage these through the app and your Apple account. Uninstalling the app from one device does not necessarily remove synchronised data or backups.

Sharing accounts: authentication information is retained while the account exists. Deleting an account requires removal of its Firebase Authentication record and associated sharing-service data; signing out or uninstalling is not an account-deletion request. Provider backup removal follows the provider’s deletion processes.

Sheets, responses and review records: Expired or revoked sheets are not currently erased automatically. They and their responses remain stored until the Worker Sharing Account is deleted or the operator removes them through an applicable administrative process. Removing a local customer record does not by itself establish that an earlier server-side sheet or exported copy has been deleted.

Technical and support records: retention depends on the time needed to operate and secure the service, investigate an issue, complete a support enquiry, resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation. Provider logs and backups have their own applicable retention processes; the expiry date of a worker link is not their deletion deadline.

You can delete your Worker Sharing Account in ClearRound Pro under Worker Sharing Account. The app requires recent authentication, revokes active links, removes owned sheets, responses, sessions and review records, and then deletes the Firebase Authentication account. If cleanup cannot be confirmed, deletion stops and can be retried. Contact mail@clearroundapp.com if the in-app process cannot be completed or for other access or deletion requests.

Deleting a sharing account is separate from deleting local/iCloud business records and cancelling an Apple subscription. Manage your subscription through your Apple account. Copies already received by a worker or exported elsewhere remain under the recipient’s control.

9. International processing

Apple, Google and their service providers operate internationally, and information may be processed outside the UK. Firebase’s applicable Data Processing and Security Terms describe its processing arrangements and transfer safeguards, including contractual clauses and UK provisions where applicable. The arrangements depend on the service and destination; this policy does not promise UK-only hosting. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

10. Security and your choices

Protect your device, Apple account and sharing account. Check each sheet before sharing, include only necessary information, choose an appropriate expiry and revoke links when no longer needed. We take reasonable steps to protect the service, but no digital system or shared link can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and exemptions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal information. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where we rely on consent. Contact us using the details above; for records controlled by a window-cleaning business, contact that business.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or another relevant supervisory authority.

12. Children and policy changes

Pro is a business-management service and is not designed for children. We may update this policy as services or practices change; the date above identifies the latest update.
