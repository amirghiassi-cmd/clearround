This policy explains how information is handled when you use ClearCarpet Pro, a business-management application being developed primarily for professional carpet cleaners, and its related services. The availability of particular features may depend on the version of the app and whether optional online services have been enabled.

Your customer and job records are your business information. If you choose to share selected jobs with workers, that information may also be processed through separate Google Firebase and Google Cloud services, not just Apple iCloud.

1. Information entered in the app

Depending on the features used, you may enter customer names, addresses, postcodes, telephone numbers, email addresses, photographs of properties, carpets or furnishings, job notes and access instructions; carpet-cleaning services and quotations; prices, job dates and schedules; payment status and methods; income, expenses, receipts and business details; and worker or team assignments. We encourage you to avoid storing unnecessary sensitive personal information in notes or photos.

You choose the information entered. The app does not require you to provide customer payment-card numbers, and you should never store card numbers or security codes in notes.

2. Who controls the information?

The carpet-cleaning business using the app generally decides why and how its customers’ and workers’ personal information is processed and is responsible for meeting its own privacy obligations, including having a lawful basis, giving appropriate notices and keeping records accurate. Where we host shared-job information on the business’s instructions, we provide processing services for that business.

GF Consultancy Services Ltd is responsible as a controller for personal information it uses for its own account administration, support, security and operation of its services.

3. Storage on devices and Apple iCloud

Business records are designed to be stored on your device using Apple’s app-data technologies. Where iCloud/CloudKit synchronisation is enabled and configured, records may also be stored and synchronised through your Apple account. Apple's services, privacy terms and device settings apply. Removing the app from one device does not necessarily remove records in iCloud or backups.

CloudKit storage and optional online Worker Sharing are separate services. An owner must select job details before sharing a sheet online.

4. Worker Sharing and account information

If Worker Sharing is enabled, the owner may register and sign in using an email address and password. Firebase Authentication may process account email, user ID, verification status, sign-in/session information and technical information required to operate and protect authentication. Workers who open a shared sheet generally do not need a separate account.

A shared sheet can include selected customer contact or address information, job instructions, service details, scheduled dates and worker assignments. If the owner enables price sharing, it may also include prices, amounts owed and payment details. Workers may submit supported outcomes, completion or postponement information, and, when enabled, payment updates for owner review.

Online services may store sharing identifiers, hashed link tokens, owner IDs, expiry and revocation settings, worker responses, review decisions and security or audit information. Anyone with an active worker-sheet link may be able to access its contents and submit responses. Only share links with the intended worker and revoke them when no longer needed. Revocation does not retrieve copies already made.

5. Providers and technical data

  • Apple: device and iCloud services, device authentication, subscriptions and App Store purchase entitlements.
  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud, where Worker Sharing is enabled: Firebase Authentication, Hosting for the worker website, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore and supporting infrastructure.
  • Services you choose to open: such as mapping, messaging, email or export destinations, which operate under their own policies.

Services may handle IP addresses, device/browser information, access times, errors, service identifiers, operational logs and similar technical information to provide, secure and troubleshoot their functions. See Firebase Privacy and Security, Google Cloud's Privacy Notice, and Apple's privacy information.

We do not sell your customer lists or use customer records for third-party behavioural advertising.

6. Purposes and lawful bases

We use information for the relevant purposes of providing app features and online sharing, maintaining accounts, handling support enquiries, securing the service, preventing misuse and complying with applicable legal obligations. Where we act as controller, the applicable bases may include performance of our contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and legal obligations. We seek consent when required for a specific optional activity. Businesses using the app must determine their own bases for processing their customers’ and workers’ information.

7. Photos, communications and device permissions

Photos, files, camera access and notifications are used only where relevant to an app feature and permitted by the device. Apple processes Face ID or other biometric verification; the app does not receive your biometric template. When you choose to call, message, email, navigate to an address or export a file, information needed for that action may go to the destination or app you select. You are responsible for securely handling exported data and for obtaining appropriate permissions before photographing a customer’s property or sending communications.

8. Purchases and business payments

Apple handles App Store purchases and subscription billing. We may receive entitlement status needed to enable app features, but not the full payment-card details used for an Apple purchase. Recording that a customer paid by cash, card or another method is a manual business record, not a payment transaction processed by ClearCarpet Pro.

9. Retention, deletion and account requests

Local and iCloud records: business records generally remain until you delete them through the app or applicable device/iCloud controls. iCloud copies and backups follow Apple's own retention and deletion processes.

Worker Sharing: if enabled, owner authentication records generally remain while the sharing account exists. Expired or revoked sheets and their responses are not necessarily automatically erased; they may remain in the service until account deletion or an applicable administrative removal. Deleting a local customer record does not by itself delete a previously shared sheet or copies received by a worker.

Where an in-app Worker Sharing Account deletion feature is available, use it to request deletion of that account and associated sharing data. If that option is unavailable or fails, contact mail@clearroundapp.com. We will explain the applicable deletion process and any verification needed. Signing out, uninstalling the app and cancelling an Apple subscription are separate actions.

Technical records and recovery copies: logs and provider-managed recovery data may be retained for different periods under the relevant service configuration and legal requirements, even after a share expires or a user requests deletion. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably necessary for handling enquiries, related issues and legal requirements.

10. International processing and security

Apple, Google and their service providers may process information in or outside the United Kingdom. Appropriate legal safeguards apply where required for international personal-data transfers. Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum and Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms describe relevant service terms; the applicable arrangements depend on the service and location. Contact us if you need details of safeguards relevant to your data.

We take reasonable measures to protect the services. You should secure your device and accounts, use a strong password, share only necessary job details, check recipients and revoke worker links when appropriate. No system or shared link can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the law and circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or receive personal information, and to object to certain processing. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Contact mail@clearroundapp.com or write to the address above. Where an individual’s data is controlled by a carpet-cleaning business using the app, the individual should normally contact that business in the first instance.

You can raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or another relevant regulator.

12. Children and policy changes

ClearCarpet Pro is intended for business users and is not designed for children. We may update this policy as app features, providers or data practices change, and will show the latest update date on this page.
