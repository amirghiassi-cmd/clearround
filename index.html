FAQ

A few quick answers.

Who is ClearRound for?

Independent window cleaners and small round-based cleaning businesses that want a straightforward way to manage customers, jobs and money.

What happens if a customer pays later?

Mark the job as Owed. It remains in your Owed list until you record payment, with the payment date kept separate from the job completion date.

Can I record one-off jobs?

Yes. A job can be recurring or one-off, so occasional gutter, fascia, conservatory or pressure-washing work can sit alongside regular window-cleaning jobs.

Can I export my figures?

Yes. Reports can be exported as a PDF or CSV file for the selected reporting period.

Does it work outside the UK?

ClearRound can display the currency associated with the region configured on the device.
